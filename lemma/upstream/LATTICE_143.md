# Lattice 143 (root system a_15 a_8) hosts no frame: a two-line proof

Lab session (Claude), 2026-10-07. Replaces, for this lattice, the "component-budget test" of 28/09 (a solver plus an exact
enumeration, never reread). Not yet refereed: Codex is asked for one pass. It does not depend on the v10 run of 143.

## Statement

**Proposition 143.** Let U be lattice 143 of the list (odd unimodular, rank 24, root system a_15 a_8; even neighbours A_15 D_9
and A_24). There are no vectors y_1, y_2, h_1..h_7, u_1..u_42 of U satisfying (R-ii) and (R-v) of Theorem R
(`AUDIT_REDUCTION.md`). In particular U hosts no frame.

Only two properties of a frame are used:
- (R-ii) the 42 vectors u_o are vectors of U of norm 3;
- (R-v) for every real v, the sum of (v,f)^2 over the 51 frame vectors f equals 7 (v,v).

The h, the y, the products between frame vectors, the new roots, T16 / T18 / T20, the pair enumeration and the search engine
are not used.

## The budget identity

Let V be a subspace of R^24 of dimension d and P_V the orthogonal projection onto it. Summing (R-v) over an orthonormal basis
e_1..e_d of V:

    sum over the 51 frame vectors f of |P_V f|^2 = sum_j sum_f (e_j,f)^2 = 7 d.          (B)

All terms are >= 0, so the 42 vectors u alone satisfy: sum_o |P_V u_o|^2 <= 7 d.

## Three facts about U (exact computation, `budget_143.py`)

1. U has 312 roots, in two classes under non-orthogonality: 72 roots of rank 8 and 240 roots of rank 15 (a_8 and a_15).
   Let W be the span of the first class, dim W = 8. For every x, |P_W x|^2 = S_8(x) / 18, where S_8(x) is the sum of (alpha,x)^2
   over the 72 roots. (For an irreducible root system of rank n with N roots of norm 2, sum of alpha alpha^T is invariant under
   the Weyl group, which acts irreducibly on the span: it is c P with c = 2 N / n by the trace; here c = 18.)
2. The vectors of U orthogonal to all roots are the multiples of one primitive vector z, and (z,z) = 144. Let l = R z, the
   orthogonal complement of the span of the roots (dimension 24 - 23 = 1). For every x, |P_l x|^2 = (x,z)^2 / 144.
3. U has 4096 vectors of norm 3 (the theta-series value: the list is complete), of three kinds:

   | (x,z)^2 | S_8(x) | \|P_W x\|^2 | \|P_l x\|^2 | vectors |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 81 | 0 | 0 | 9/16 | 1120 |
   | 169 | 16 | 8/9 | 169/144 | 288 |
   | 9 | 36 | 2 | 1/16 | 2688 |

## Proof of Proposition 143

Let c be the number of u_o of the third kind ((u,z)^2 = 9). By (B) for V = W: 2 c <= 7 * 8 = 56, so c <= 28.
The other u_o, at least 42 - 28 = 14 of them, are of the first two kinds and have |P_l u|^2 >= 9/16. By (B) for V = l:

    7 = 7 * 1 >= sum_o |P_l u_o|^2 >= 14 * 9/16 = 63/8 > 7.

Contradiction. QED

## Cross-check of the table by hand (not needed for the proof)

M = A_15 + A_8 is the lattice generated by the roots, det M = 16 * 9 = 144. By fact 2 and unimodularity, the primitive closure
of M in U has determinant (z,z) = 144, so M is primitive, and U / (M + Z z) is cyclic of order 144. Every x of U is
x = x_15 + x_8 + (t/144) z with t = (x,z), x_15 in the class u t of A_15^*/A_15 = Z/16 and x_8 in the class v t of
A_8^*/A_8 = Z/9, for fixed units u, v. The norms in the class i of A_n^* are i (n+1-i)/(n+1) + 2k, k >= 0.
Integrality of the norm for t = 1 gives 9 u^2 + 16 v^2 = 1 (mod 144): u = +-3 or +-5 (mod 16), v = +-2 (mod 9). With u = +-3
the class t = 5 would contain a vector of norm 15/16 + 8/9 + 25/144 = 2, a root outside M: impossible. So u = +-5, v = +-2.
Norm 3 with |t| <= 20 then has exactly the solutions
- t = +-3: classes +-1 and +-3, 15/16 + 2 + 1/16: 2 * 16 * 84 = 2688 vectors;
- t = +-9: classes +-3 and 0, 39/16 + 0 + 9/16: 2 * 560 = 1120 vectors;
- t = +-13: classes +-1 and +-1, 15/16 + 8/9 + 169/144: 2 * 16 * 9 = 288 vectors;

(every other t gives a minimum above 3, or a norm of the wrong parity: t = 10 and t = 16 give norm 4, t = 19 norm 5), in all
4096. This reproduces the table, counts included.

## Why the packing criterion does not settle 143

C1's packing test (`c1/code/envelope_pack.py`): m(x) = r/8 - E(x) with r = 312 and E(x) = sum over the positive roots of
(alpha,x)^2 = 9 |P_W x|^2 + 16 |P_{a_15} x|^2. For the three kinds: E = 39, 23, 33, so m = 0, 16, 6. There are 1120 vectors
with m = 0, so the 42 smallest values of m sum to 0, against 24 * min(16, 25) - 312 = 72 new roots: the test passes trivially.
The tracker field "packing: survives" of 28/09 is consistent with this.
