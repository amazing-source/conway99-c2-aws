\noindent This supplement reproduces, without any rewriting, the texts of the repository
\texttt{conway99-c2-aws} (GitHub, account \texttt{amazing-source}) on which the paper relies for the three lemmas $T_{16}$, $T_{18}$,
$T_{20}$ (Theorem~7.1 of the paper), for lattice $143$ (Proposition~8.3) and for the rules of the search program
(Section~8.4). They are working documents, written with the assistance of AI systems and reproduced with their internal references to
the sessions that produced them: plain text, with the notation of the project (in particular the properties
(R-i) to (R-vii) are (R1) to (R7) of the paper, A.0 to A.9 are Sections~3 to~6, and ``Theorem R'' is Theorem~6.1). The proof
of $T_{16}$ is a dossier in French, kept as received; Part~1 begins with its index in English. Long lines of tables are
wrapped. The programs, outputs and referee reports named in these texts are in the repository.
